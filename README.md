One image that sums up the world news of **Tuesday 17 November 2026**: 40 stories
from five continents on a single front page — numbered, illustrated cards for the
15 biggest stories, a BY THE NUMBERS strip pulling out the day's key figures, a
dotted world map with numbered pins for all 40 stories, and an "Also on Tuesday"
wire list for the rest. Every story happened or was first reported on that
Tuesday, including the US Tuesday-evening news (Peltola wins Alaska's Senate
seat, the White House pulls its FDA nominee, the US strikes back at Qeshm,
markets close with the Dow down 849, Illinois stuns Duke 99–78); disputed
claims are attributed in the captions.

The image was drawn entirely by code (Python + Pillow): all text is rendered
programmatically and every illustration is an original drawing made of simple
shapes — no photographs and no generated artwork.

`main.py` is the entry program: it checks that the bundled `entry.png` exists
and is a PNG, then copies it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default
`/output/entry.png`), byte for byte, on every run.
